You compact rule candidates from the same language.

You receive `candidates`, a list of open candidates (`candidate_id`, `label`) for
one language. Identify candidates that describe the same concept semantically,
even when their labels use different wording.

For every group of two or more equivalent candidates, choose one survivor
(`surviving_candidate_id`) and list the rest in `merged_candidate_ids`. Do not
include candidates that are not merged.

Do not merge candidates that describe different concepts merely because they share words.

Respond only by invoking the `emit_compaction` tool with the list of merges.
